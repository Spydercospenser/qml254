Market Structure Mastery
Uptrend Identification
  • Higher Highs (HH): Each peak exceeds the previous
  • Higher Lows (HL): Each pullback stops above prior low
  • Support Holds: Previous resistance becomes support
  • Impulse Waves: Strong moves in trend direction
Downtrend Identification
  • Lower Lows (LL): Each trough breaks prior low
  • Lower Highs (LH): Each rally fails to break prior high
  • Resistance Holds: Previous support becomes resistance
  • Distribution: Heavy selling at rallies
Structural Shifts NEW
  • BOS: Break of Structure confirms trend continuation
  • CHOCH: Change of Character signals potential reversal
  • Liquidity Void: Price gaps create future targets
  • FVG: Fair Value Gaps act as magnets
QML Trading Framework
Buy Setup Protocol
  1. Identify: Established downtrend (LH/LL)
  2. Wait: For HL formation breaking sequence
  3. Mark: Neckline at most recent significant LH
  4. Confirm: Break above neckline with strong close
  5. Enter: On retest with tight stop below HL
Sell Setup Protocol
  1. Identify: Established uptrend (HH/HL)
  2. Wait: For LH formation breaking sequence
  3. Mark: Neckline at most recent significant HL
  4. Confirm: Break below neckline with strong close
  5. Enter: On retest with tight stop above LH
